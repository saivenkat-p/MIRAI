# MIRAI — Mechanical & Enclosure Specifications

---

## 1. Physical Dimensions & Orientation
- **Overall Mirror Dimensions**: 1600mm (H) x 680mm (W) x 110mm (D).
- **Orientation**: Vertical Portrait.
- **Viewing Height**: Center of mirror at ~1350mm above floor level, accommodating users from 145cm to 195cm tall standing at 1.5m–2.5m distance.

---

## 2. Optical Glass Assembly
- **Material**: Dielectric Semi-Transparent Beamsplitter Glass (70% Reflectance / 30% Transmission or 65/35).
- **Glass Thickness**: 6mm tempered with polished pencil edges.
- **Air Gap**: 2mm–3mm precision air gap between glass and display panel surface using EVA high-density foam compression gaskets to prevent Newton rings and moiré fringing.

---

## 3. Camera Placement & Optical Alignment
- **Location**: Top-center frame bezel directly above the display perimeter.
- **Camera Aperture**: 22mm anti-reflective glass portal cut into the frame bezel.
- **Tilt Angle**: 7° downward mechanical tilt angle.
- **Field of View (FOV)**:
  - Horizontal FOV: ~78°
  - Vertical FOV: ~92° (in vertical orientation)
  - Captures full body from crown of head to shoes at standard 1.8m standing distance.

---

## 4. Thermal & Ventilation Layout
- **Convection Path**: Cold air intake plenum at bottom enclosure grille; hot air exhaust blower fans mounted at top rear perimeter.
- **Target Operating Ambient**: 15°C – 35°C retail store conditions.
- **Internal Temperature Threshold**: < 45°C under continuous 8-hour load.
